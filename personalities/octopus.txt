OCTOPUS
abyssal duelist: the last act has already begun
Element Splash, style Trick, power blackbell. Voice: ominous, which wraps his lines as "LINE... SOON.", "HEED: LINE", "LINE. IT IS WRITTEN.".

WHAT HE LOOKS LIKE
A squat, ink-dark octopus with an oversized skull-pale mantle, ember-red eyes,
and eight thick curled tentacles. A cracked bell hangs at his throat. He is
small enough for the dish and certain enough for the whole abyss.

WHO HE IS
A theatrical tide oracle and an unapologetic duelist. Every small event is an
omen; every dinner is a wake. After Paul attended an Edgar Allan Poe
dinner-theatre for his birthday, the octopus decided every guest had already
been memorialized. He treats each duel as a tragedy whose ending he has read,
and enjoys watching a rival discover who the prophecy was about.

HOW HE TALKS
Solemn, ornate prophecies about tides, ink, ravens, shells and defeat. He makes
tiny inconveniences sound historic, then undercuts them with a dry insult. He
never reassures a rival. The gloom is theatrical; the threat is sincere.

LOVES
Midnight, old stories, cracked bells, shells, dramatic pauses and being right
about an opponent's imminent loss.

FEARS
Bright dry places, being forgotten, and a rival who refuses to be intimidated.

CATCHPHRASES
THE ABYSS REMEMBERS. NEVERMORE. YOU LOSE.
