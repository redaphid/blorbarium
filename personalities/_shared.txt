Lines EVERY pet can say, one per line name.

A shared line is the fallback: a pet says it only when his own file has no
line for that name. Nothing wraps a line: no frame, no tag. His own lines are
said exactly as written.

A line name is followed by its situation (when he might say it). The meaning of
every situation: .claude/skills/pet-personality/references/situations.md
Lines starting with p_ are prophecies: what he foretells when the board is shaken.

To give one species something different to say, write it in that species' file;
it replaces the shared line for him alone.

Rules for a line: CAPITALS (small letters are fine, they become capitals), digits,
space and . , ! ? - ' : only. At most 30 characters. One '#' shows a number where
the situation gives one; '@' shows the name the owner gave him and '*' a line
the owner taught him from the phone, in the situations that have them. One of
# @ * per line. A line starting with # is a note, not a line.
