# On-device e2e on badge128_pm, and controls with no button (2026-10-19)

## 1. The e2e on the new platform

`badge128_pm` (Arduino 2.0.17 rebuilt with power management, light sleep in
low power) flashed from this Linux host onto both badges on the cable:
badge 1 (mossby, 5C94095000, d4:05:92:77:1b:3c) and the test board
5C94094999 (d4:05:92:77:1b:44, an egg, BLE name puffkin). The second board
was unplugged partway, so the two-badge `tools/duel_soak.py` was **not**
rerun on `badge128_pm`; it is still owed.

| Run | Badge | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `tools/power_e2e.py` | 5C94094999 (egg) | 4/9 at first (every failure the script's, below); after the fixes 8/9, `hold` failing on a typo in its new egg skip, since fixed but not rerun (the board was unplugged) |
| `tools/power_e2e.py` | badge 1 | 9/9, twice (before and after the controls change) |
| `tools/debug_ble_e2e.py` | badge 1 | 130/130, then 139/139 with SIDE and KNOCK |
| `tools/gesture_e2e.py` (new) | badge 1 | 7/7 |

Fixed on the way:

- `power_e2e.py` judged "the pet lived on while dark" by the creature's
  `age=`, which an egg never has: the `[hw] up=` line now carries `tick=`
  (the Dish's tick, any occupant) and the script reads it.
- A failed scenario left the badge low, so the next one failed too (the
  `ble` scenario on the egg board): each failure now ends with
  `DEBUG power wake`.
- `hold` on an egg now says it is skipped instead of failing on
  `debug sleepiness: no creature`.
- `tools/flash_board.sh` refused to flash on Linux because PlatformIO's
  esptool is 4.5; it now takes the first esptool of 4.7 or newer
  (`$ESPTOOL`, PlatformIO's, then PATH).
- `DEBUG hatch` on the cable (the debug link's HATCH, with FREEHATCH), so a
  script can hatch an egg with no phone. Its first version reported `ok`
  over an egg that waited for a phone and warped four pet hours; it now
  sets FREEHATCH first and says `no egg` unless he hatched.

## 2. The controls

Found while reading the code: with BOOT covered by the case, **no food could
reach the pet at all** (a pellet was only ever a BOOT press; the phone is
refused plain food on purpose), and nothing could tuck him in, keep an egg,
pick Mortal, throw a special, Finish or ask for a rematch.

The design, its reasons and the tests are in `docs/controls.md`. In one
line: knock knock = press, turn onto a side and hold = hold, the hang =
double press, each firing the button's own stimulus so every `kBoot` row
works with no button.

Found on the hardware, not in the native tests: the settled rotation lags a
turn back by ~1.5 s, so a 1 s turn onto a side held at 2.4 s, after the
badge was upright again. Fixed in both `SideHoldDetector` and
`HangDetector` (gravity in the screen's frame must be within ~45 degrees of
down), with native tests for both.

Behaviour that changed for every pet (owner, please confirm):

- A knock knock now drops a pellet (and steps, peeks, asks...), everywhere
  a press does.
- In a clutch a knock knock used to keep the egg; it now moves to the next
  one. Keeping is a hold.
- In a duel turn a knock knock throws a quick special when one fits; it
  still cheers otherwise.
- Holding the badge sideways for ~2 s tucks him in (the hold's meaning
  when awake). One per turn.
- New lines: `KNOCK KNOCK ON ME FOR SNACKS` (hungry, any age, every 30 min
  at most) and `TIP ME SIDEWAYS. NIGHT NIGHT` (first day). `no_hat`'s
  cooldown went from 30 to 60 min: the extra rows moved the seeded draw and
  it came back 7 times in 6 h, past the test's once-an-hour rule.

No save format change. The firmware version is still 0.7.11.

## Later the same day: real hands (test board 5C94094999)

`DEBUG trace` and `tools/imu_record.py` recorded the owner doing each gesture
while marks named the steps; `tools/imu_analyze.py` set what the IMU did
against what fired. Findings, in order, each fixed and pushed:

1. The tap engine missed 5/5 deliberate knock knocks and reported ~34 double
   taps from picking up, turning, carrying and pocketing (each a pellet).
   Replaced by `KnockSense` on the raw samples, then tuned live four times
   (soft knocks between samples, rim knocks, quick 0.12 s pairs, a soft
   first knock, an unsteady grip).
2. Every knock knock also fired a shake (the bounce in the hand): the shake
   now ignores a knock burst (`BodySample::knocking`).
3. Side holds: 8/8 turns held once each; the release lag fix worked
   (released 0.25 s after the turn back, the panel 0.8 s later).
4. The hang fired ~1.8 s after the turn; a sleepy pet woken for it dozed off
   and fight mode closed itself ~9 s later (still so: owner to decide).
   Held upside down in a pocket it fires too; the owner accepts that.
5. The IMU's z points into the screen: face up read -1 g. Fixed.
6. Table knocks need the 250 Hz reads: a knock on a lying badge is over in
   ~10 ms. Desk noise (typing, cups, bumps) rattles 60 ms and more.
7. Face down: asleep at 91 s (desk vibration restarts the 30 s stillness
   wait), low power at 148 s, woke on the flip.

Owner decisions taken: occasional false hangs are fine; face down always
sleeps and goes low in 2 min; a pose fires once when laid down.
