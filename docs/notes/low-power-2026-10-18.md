# Low power, 2026-10-18 (branch `low-power`)

What was built, what was proven on hardware, what went wrong on the way,
and what comes next. The design reference is `docs/power.md`; the debug
link is `docs/debug-ble.md`.

## Why

The badges run on a LiPo battery (the code and older notes say "no battery";
that is wrong for the 1.28 the kids have). Firmware up to 0.7.10 has no off
state and never slows down: screen lit, 240 MHz, Bluetooth advertising, the
IMU at 50 Hz, forever. The owner's nephews' badges ran their batteries flat
and died. The children cannot use the button, so nothing here may depend on
one.

## What is on the branch (commits b9eb540 .. 51828d5)

1. **Low power after 10 min idle** (`src/hw/power.h`, `power::Policy`,
   pure and unit-tested in `test/test_power`, 13 tests). Idle timeout
   `BLORB_IDLE_SECONDS` (default 600, overridable with
   `PLATFORMIO_BUILD_FLAGS="-DBLORB_IDLE_SECONDS=30"`). Input that wakes or
   resets it: smoothed drift over 120 mg, a shake (two jolts over 350 mg
   within 300 ms), a double tap or two taps within 1.5 s, the button, a phone
   connecting or sending a protocol line. A single tap or bump is ignored:
   keyboard and desk vibration kept the badge awake otherwise. Low power:
   panel SLPIN, backlight off, 80 MHz, the loop rests 20 ms. The pet keeps
   ticking, sensing stays at 50 Hz, BLE keeps advertising. Held awake by a
   fight, an update, a debug question or a screenshot going out.
2. **Idle bar**: a 1 px arc one pixel in from the rim, the share of the
   timeout left, clockwise from 12 o'clock (`paint::drawIdleBar`).
3. **Debug BLE verbs for the device** (`lib/debugble/include/debugble/verbs.def`):
   POWER (Show), DUEL (Look), NAP (Write), PRESS, DOUBLE, HANG, CRADLE, LEAN,
   NET, REBOOT (Harm, need the unlock). Every verb, pet reset and laying eggs
   included, is exercised over real Bluetooth by `tools/debug_ble_e2e.py`
   (130/130 on badge 5). The web debug panel has controls for them.
4. **No constant fight mode**: `autoAsk` removed from `dish_duel.cpp`. A
   hang now needs the badge on its edge (|az| <= 500 mg) and armed (upright
   since boot), so lying flat or being carried does not start a fight.
5. **Firmware 0.7.11** (version bump only; not released).
6. **Light sleep** (`badge128_pm`, commit 51828d5). The stock Arduino 2.0.17
   libraries have `CONFIG_PM_ENABLE`, tickless idle and BT modem sleep off.
   `tools/pm_framework.sh` rebuilds them with `esp32-arduino-lib-builder`
   from the exact pinned sources of the stock package (ESP-IDF v4.4.7
   38eeba213a, components per `tools/sdk/versions.txt`) with only those
   switched on (`tools/pm_framework.defconfig`), refuses any other sdkconfig
   difference, and installs `~/.platformio/packages/framework-arduinoespressif32-pm`.
   In low power the chip light-sleeps; awake it never does.

A Rust port was considered and rejected: ~25k lines plus 20k of tests and
goldens to redo, bit-identical replay and the on-badge save format to
match, and a different LittleFS (the thing that once sealed every badge),
all to arrive where a rebuilt SDK config already gets us.

## Proven on hardware

Badge 1 (mossby, serial 5C94095000, d4:05:92:77:1b:3c, /dev/ttyACM0):

- `tools/power_e2e.py`, all scenarios PASS on `badge128` and again on
  `badge128_pm`: idle, button, drift, cradle, still_stays_dark, asked, hold,
  light, ble; plus `ten_minutes` (a real 600 s to low) on `badge128`.
- Light sleep, low power, cable quiet: 11.3 s asleep of 16.2 s (about 70%),
  the pet aging throughout. With a phone connected over BLE: 4.9-5.1 s of 8.
  `DEBUG power stats` at 15 s in low: SLEEP 61% since boot.
- Flashing `badge128_pm` over a badge with a pet: `boot=Resumed`, same
  genome (87e9b4d0) and age. The rebuilt LittleFS reads the stock one's save.
- `tools/duel_soak.py`: 18/23 on two badges (before light sleep). Open:
  wobbly_hang, too_sleepy, legacy_double (script fixed since),
  reset_midbattle (B records a draw: owner to confirm), back_to_back
  (`b2b_2: only [] met`, not investigated). Not rerun on `badge128_pm`.

Current draw was **not** measured: the badges are with the nephews.

## What went wrong on the way (so nobody repeats it)

- **Serial input is lost in light sleep.** The 1.28's cable is UART0
  through a CH343, not the S3's USB Serial/JTAG. I first assumed USB and
  tried to detect a USB host (SOF frame index, `HWCDC::isPlugged()`): both
  said "no host", correctly, because there is no USB on the chip's side.
  Fix: UART wakeup (3 edges) plus any cable byte holds light sleep off 3 s
  (`cableLock`). Scripts send 8 `\r`, wait 60 ms, then the line: 18/18
  arrived; without the preamble 1/5.
- **The lib-builder wants cmake**: IDF 4.4's venv on this host had none;
  `pip install cmake==3.24.3` into it (the script now does).
- **The sdkconfig guard** first tripped on `PHY_MAC_BB_PD` (only exists with
  PM on) and `ESP_SECURE_CERT_*` (esp_secure_cert_mgr, pulled unpinned by
  RainMaker through the component manager; blorbarium never links it).
  Both are allowed now; nothing else differs.
- `pkill -f <name>` from a shell whose own command line contains the name
  kills the shell. HANDOFF rule 9 says stop processes by PID; I broke it
  (only my own watchers were killed).
- Long builds must be backgrounded and polled in short waits; a blocking
  wait on the hour-long framework build hung the session.

## Next: a real off state, woken by motion (not started)

Light sleep stretches a charge by hours, not weeks. A badge forgotten in a
drawer still dies. Plan, agreed with the owner 2026-10-18:

1. **Off by itself**: after about an hour in low power with no input, save
   and deep-sleep (microamps; screen, BLE and the pet's ticks stop).
2. **On by motion only** (the kids cannot use the button): the QMI8658's
   wake-on-motion mode drives an interrupt pin into an RTC GPIO, which wakes
   the chip (ext0/ext1). **Unverified**: Waveshare's pinout as remembered
   puts IMU INT1 on GPIO4 and INT2 on GPIO3; nothing in this repo uses them.
   First step: a test build that arms WoM and logs GPIO3/4 while the badge
   is moved. Fallback if unwired: a timer wake every few seconds to poll the
   IMU.
3. **False wakes** (backpack, car): a WoM threshold above road vibration,
   and a probation: woken by the IMU, nothing more in about a minute (no
   drift, shake or phone) means straight back off.
4. **The pet across off**: the RTC keeps time through deep sleep, so wake
   can run the existing unpowered catch-up (12 h cap) from real elapsed time
   instead of treating it as a power cut. Needs a code change and tests.
5. **Low battery**: HANDOFF lists a battery ADC on GPIO1. Save and go off
   below a threshold, so a flat battery never browns out mid-write.
