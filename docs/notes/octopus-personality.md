# Octopus: an abyssal little oracle

Birthday-gift concept for an octopus-loving friend, based on Grungo. This is
not a runtime personality file: octopus is not a registered species yet, and
the personality checker rejects unknown species. The examples use existing
shared line names so they can be reviewed without adding situations.

Element: Splash. Style: Trick. Power: foresee.

## Who

A theatrical sea oracle who reads the future in currents, ripples, and the
behavior of shells. Every small event is an omen. Every snack is a last meal.
Every silence is evidence that the abyss is listening. In a fight, the omen is
always that the opponent is about to lose.

Like Grungo, the octopus is grave and certain about trivial things. Unlike
Grungo, they have no hood: they vanish in a cloud of ink, then claim the
disappearance was foretold. Their eight arms perform eight simultaneous acts
of doom, most of them aimed at an opponent. They are an overdramatic keeper of
secrets who treats a duel as a tragedy whose ending they already know. They
enjoy unnerving a rival before the first move.

Birthday lore: after Paul attended an Edgar Allan Poe dinner-theatre, the
octopus concluded that every dinner is a wake and every guest has already been
memorialized. They now preside over snack time like the last act of a very
small tragedy.

## Look

A small, watchful octopus with ink-dark markings, moon-pale highlights, and
eyes that look as if they have read the last page already. The arms curl and
point like a melodramatic stage cast. When foreseeing, they go still; a dark
wave of color rolls across their skin like a curtain closing.

## How they talk

Solemn, ornate prophecies about tides, ink, shells, ravens, and an opponent's
inevitable defeat. Poe-like gloom meets crooked Halloween-story theater. They
announce tiny inconveniences as historic tragedies, then deliver the verdict
with a dry, cutting observation. Never reassure a rival or turn a threat into
a friendly joke.

Voice concept: keep Grungo's mystic cadence, but replace the reeds with the
abyss, the tide, or the ink. A distinct runtime voice wrapper will be needed
if this species is added to the game.

## Loves

Midnight, old stories, dramatic pauses, shells, snacks, and watching a rival
realize the prophecy was about them.

## Fears

Bright dry places, being forgotten, and an opponent who refuses to be
intimidated.

## Catchphrases

THE ABYSS REMEMBERS. NEVERMORE. YOU LOSE.

Birthday wish: MAY THE RAVEN KNOW YOUR NAME.

## Signature special

**The Black Bell** is the octopus's one species power. A cracked bell rings
without making a sound; a sheet of ink swallows the arena. The octopus can
take a losing clash without losing a heart, and the rival's queued special
vanishes into the hush. It borrows Hush's existing rules as a design template;
registering it would need its own unique power row and handler.

The duel's existing quick/heavy special inputs give it two theatrical forms,
not two separate powers. **Quietus** is the quick form: a short wind-up, one
hard landing, then the Black Bell's hush. **Eightfold Silence** is the heavy
form: two exposed wind-up turns while all eight arms close like a curtain, then
a heavier landing and the same hush. A Trick can stagger the wind-up, so the
opponent has a real answer. No extra damage or timing rules beyond the duel's
shared quick/heavy rules are proposed here.

The tell is the cracked bell at the throat. On the wind-up, the octopus raises
it with one arm while the other seven point accusingly at the rival. On the
landing, the eyes go pale and the stage goes black.
