# Grungo stories

Five stories about the blorbarium we want. None of this exists yet. Each story is told from Peter's side, across days or generations. Each ends with what it does to grungo and his line, and with the mechanics the design needs to make it real. The screen mockups are composites of grungo's real art on the 240x240 round dish.

How the device works in these stories. The round screen is the dish. Peter can tilt it, shake it, knock on the case, cradle it, or lay it face down. There is one button on the back. The phone is optional. A phone tap opens a web page that talks to the device over Bluetooth and adds twists.

## 1. The breakfast bell

*Learning and tricks, owner and routine, physical play (the button), lineage (an heirloom).*

![Waiting for the bell.](illus/s1.png)

![07:36. Grungo waits at the bell before it rings.](img/s1a.png)

Peter makes coffee at about 7:40. On the first morning of this life he clicks the button on the back of the case once. A small brass bell rings at the edge of the dish. Nothing falls. Then he holds the button for a second, and a pellet drops by the bell. Grungo has been sitting in the middle of the dish like a stone. He walks over, eats, and walks back to the middle.

A click is the bell. A hold is the food. Peter keeps the order the same every morning: click, wait, hold.

On day two the bell makes grungo jump. "eep! a bell. grungo foresaw the bell." He eats when the pellet comes, but he doesn't connect the two.

On day four he does. The bell rings, and he turns and waddles to the rim before the pellet falls. Peter knocks twice on the case when he gets there. In this house a double knock means *good frog*.

By day six he is ahead of Peter. At 7:36 he leaves the middle of the dish, sits by the bell with his eager face, and waits. When Peter picks up the device the marquee says "brrrup. breakfast is FORESEEN. any minute." He is right, and smug.

Peter adds a trick. Two clicks means spin. The first time, two clicks gets a confused blink. Peter waits until grungo hops in a circle on his own, which he does when he's pleased, and double-knocks. It takes most of a week, and a few knocks land on the wrong thing. By the second Sunday, two clicks gets a spin with the cloak flapping, about two times in three.

He also picks up a habit nobody taught him. One morning he hopped twice just before the pellet fell. Now he does a lucky double hop at the bell every morning. Peter never rewards it. Grungo is sure it works.

![Saturday, 08:52. Peter slept in.](img/s1b.png)

On Saturday Peter sleeps in. Grungo is at the bell at 7:36. He is still there at 8:00. At 8:52 Peter rolls over and picks him up, and grungo has his annoyed face on. The marquee reads "grungo is not upset. grungo is a little upset." Peter rings and holds. Grungo eats, does the lucky hop, and forgives him: "croooak. this was tomorrow peter's problem. tomorrow is now."

The lesson lasts. Whole nights pass and grungo still knows the bell hour the next morning.

He dies of old age on day eleven and lays an egg. The froglet that hatches has never heard the bell. On his first morning he walks to the rim at 7:41, sits by the bell and looks up. The family tree on Peter's phone says "heirloom: the bell hour (from gen I)". The spin did not pass on.

![Gen II, first morning, at the bell without being taught.](img/s1c.png)

**Impact on him and his line.** This grungo learned Peter's morning, one trick on a cue, and one superstition. His line now hatches knowing that breakfast comes at the bell at about 7:40. If Peter's routine changes, the heirloom fades over a few generations of not being paid, the way an instinct should.

**What this demands of the design.**

- The button is a bell. A click is a cue with no food in it, and a hold drops a pellet. One click and two clicks are separate stimuli the brain can see.
- Stimuli that interrupt the current action, and a heritable memory span of a few seconds, so a double knock after the walk to the rim credits the walk (depth proposal 3).
- Hour bins and a Beg action that pays when a pellet drops, so waiting for 7:40 can emerge (proposal 7).
- Forgetting that scales with the weight, instead of a flat 0.14 off every weight each night, so a week's lesson outlasts the night (e2e root cause 1).
- Heirlooms ranked by what this life learned, |w - w_birth|, so the bell hour passes on and a trick taught to a cue mostly doesn't (proposal 2).
- Credit for the last few actions, not just the last one. The lucky double hop comes from there (proposal 9).

## 2. Shake him and ask

*Prediction and visions, personality and voice, genetics (oracle and voice genes), physical play (the shake).*

![The bubbles talking.](illus/s2.png)

Peter can't decide whether to go out to a show tonight. He picks up the device and shakes it.

Most of the time a shake is just a shake. Grungo's eyes go huge and he jumps a full body height off the floor of the dish. He lands with his hood pulled tight, and the marquee says "eep! ...grungo knew you would do that." That is the hop, and in this grungo it happens three times out of four.

![Most shakes: the hop.](img/s2a.png)

The fourth time is different. He doesn't jump. He goes still. A teal ring lights around both eyes, pulses, and throws off small sparks. A bubble rises from his head with a picture in it: a hand knocking, and "+ 9 min". The marquee says "a knock. soon. it is WRITTEN."

![One shake in four: a vision.](img/s2b.png)

Peter laughs, because he didn't ask about knocking. Nine minutes later he sits down at his desk and taps the case twice to say hello, as he does every day. The bubble pops back up with a small gold check, and grungo hops in a circle. "croooak. WRITTEN. grungo is never wrong."

The visions are real predictions. Grungo keeps a running guess about what comes next in his world, learned from what usually follows what. The knock comes after Peter sits down. The bell comes at the breakfast hour. The dark comes when Peter lays him face down at night. When the oracle fires, he shows his strongest guess and a time window, and later he checks it. After a few weeks Peter notices that grungo is right more often than chance, and most often about Peter. "the dark. soon." at 22:50 means grungo has learned when Peter goes to bed.

He is wrong too, in character. When the knock doesn't come, the bubble returns with a smudge on it: "grungo meant a different knock." With no confident guess at all, he improvises, gravely. Peter asks whether his van will start. Grungo glows, thinks, and announces "brrrup. the bubbles see azerbaijan. the van will start THERE."

Grungo never answers the question Peter asked. Peter asks about the show and grungo foresees a snack. Peter goes to the show.

Shaking him too much stops being fun for him. Five shakes in a minute and the hops get jumpier and the hood stays up. The oracle won't fire for an hour: "the bubbles are tired. ask later, brother." He learns that shaking is unpleasant.

The oracle runs in the family, and it drifts. Three generations on, Peter picks an egg because its shell has a violet sheen. The grungo who hatches has violet foresight instead of teal. She sees visions on one shake in two. Her voice has mutated too. She whispers and hedges, her thinking sound is "hmmmm" instead of "brrrup", and she has lost the big capital word. She mostly foresees food.

![Gen III: violet sight, a whisper, and a wart.](img/s2c.png)

**Impact on him and his line.** In gen I the oracle fires a quarter of the time. By gen III it fires half the time, and Peter caused that by picking glowing eggs. What the line foresees, and how it says so, drifts with mutation, so each line ends up with its own seer.

**What this demands of the design.**

- The shake forks on a heritable oracle chance: prophecy or hop. Shakes close together raise stress, switch the oracle off for an hour, and teach him that shaking is unpleasant.
- An expectation table that predicts the next stimulus within a time window from the current situation (proposal 6). The vision shows the top prediction and its window, and a later check marks it kept or missed.
- A pictogram set for visions: knock, bell, pellet, marble, dark, egg, phone. Low-confidence visions draw from a small corpus of absurd bits.
- A phrase grammar driven by voice genes: the thinking sound, the capital word, hedging, length, and which topics he prefers. Every line goes through his Never list.
- A kept prophecy is a stimulus that makes him proud. Hit rate is a lifetime stat in the lineage.
- Halo colour and strength come from the glow genes.

## 3. Marble season

*Minigames, physical play (tilt, marble, knock, button), learning, moving around the dish.*

![Keeper.](illus/s3.png)

Peter double-presses the button and a game wheel slides out along the bottom rim. It has three icons: a marble, a fly and the bell. He tilts the device to move the light between them and knocks once to pick.

![The game wheel. Tilt to pick, knock to play.](img/s3c.png)

Keeper is the marble game. A blue glass marble rolls wherever the dish tilts, at real speed, and bounces off the rim. A red arc on the bottom rim is the pond. Peter tilts to roll the marble into the pond, and grungo tries to stop it.

On day one he is bad at it. He watches the marble, not the tilt, so he is always a step late. When it zips past him he makes his alarmed face and says "eep". Two saves out of ten. He doesn't mind. Playing feeds his boredom and his curiosity whether he wins or not.

![Day one. Two saves out of ten.](img/s3a.png)

By day three he watches the tilt instead. When Peter's wrist starts to dip left, grungo is already walking left. He stops chasing the marble and starts guarding the side the dish leans toward. Five out of ten.

Peter starts cheating and banks shots off the rim. That works for two days. Then grungo learns the bounces too, because a bounce surprised him, and surprises are what he likes most. By day six he saves seven out of ten. After one save he does something Peter never taught him. He shoves the marble back uphill to Peter's side and sits and waits. "your turn. grungo has seen all your tilts." He has invented fetch. When Peter finally gets one past him, grungo is gracious about it: "duuuude. a good tilt. grungo allowed it."

![Day six. Seven saves, and he sends it back.](img/s3b.png)

Fly snap is the second game. A fly loops around the dish, and Peter clicks the button when it crosses grungo's tongue line. Grungo watches Peter's timing. After a week he starts snapping on his own, a little early, and he is better at it than Peter. Every fly he catches takes a little off his hunger, so a long game is also lunch. The third icon is the bell drill from the morning trick.

He gets bored of games he has solved. Once Peter's tilts stop surprising him, keeper stops paying. Grungo walks back to the middle of the dish and sits like a stone. "grungo has seen this one." The fix is a new game or a sneakier Peter.

He moves around the dish when he is doing something. He rests in the middle. In keeper he patrols the bottom rim, and in fly snap he sits under the fly's loop. When the game ends he walks back to the centre.

**Impact on him and his line.** This grungo became a good keeper. He passes on *watch the tilt* as an heirloom, so his hatchling leans toward the downhill side from the first day. Game scores go in the lineage book, so Peter can compare lines. One line is good at keeper. Another line is good at catching flies.

**What this demands of the design.**

- A game registry with one row and one handler per game. A double press opens it, tilt picks and a knock starts. Play feeds drives and learning like any other stimulus.
- Marble physics from the IMU at 50 Hz, with rim bounces and a goal arc.
- Tilt direction and marble speed as brain features, so he can learn to read the tilt and get there first.
- Curiosity from surprise (proposal 8). A game he can fully predict stops relieving boredom, and a heritable curiosity gene sets how fast a game goes stale.
- Per-game scores for each life, shown on the rim during play and kept in the lineage book.
- He rests at the centre, and each game names the positions he walks to while it runs.

## 4. Seven grungos

*Genetics and visible mutation (decals, clothes, the egg pick), death and lineage.*

![The Wartking and his egg.](illus/s4.png)

![Seven generations of one line.](img/s4a.png)

The first grungo is the plain one: olive skin, a brown cloak, dark red eyes. He lives eleven days, keeps Peter company, sees small futures, and dies of old age in his sleep. His body becomes a small brown lump with closed eyes, and an egg glows beside it.

Gen II hatches with a wart on his forehead. The gene diff on Peter's phone says "warts: new mutation, dominant". Peter likes the wart.

That is when the game changes. At the end of his life gen II lays a clutch of three eggs, and Peter gets to choose. The shells hint at what is inside: one speckled, one with a rust tint, one plain. Peter tilts to move a teal marker between them and knocks to keep one. The other two sink back into the bog. He picks the speckled one with the rust tint.

![Gen II's clutch. Peter picks.](img/s4b.png)

Gen III has three warts and a rust cloak. Gen IV comes from the wartiest egg of the next clutch. He has six warts, and Peter calls him the Wartking. The Wartking hoards. He picks up every pebble and bit of scrap that turns up in the dish. On day five he is wearing one, a grey pebble on a string around his neck. His hoard has become his clothes. "six warts. one pebble. a very good frog."

![Gen IV, the Wartking.](img/s4d.png)

Gen V hatches with a small glowing mushroom growing out of his hood. The phone marks the rare mutation with a star. Peter loves him. On day two Peter trips getting off a bus and drops the case on the pavement. He drops it again picking it up. Grungo wears his alarmed face for a long time, and by evening he is moving slowly. At 14:10 on day three he dies. The run is over early. The screen shows the brown lump, the mushroom still faintly glowing, and the egg beside it. "grungo saw this. the egg is for later. everything is."

![Gen V. A short run.](img/s4c.png)

Peter is upset for an hour. Then he checks the phone. Five generations is a feat, and the lineage has a new line: "fifth generation: this line now foresees a shake coming." A run that ends badly still moves the line forward.

Gen VI hatches from that egg. The mushroom is gone, because it was recessive and this egg paired it with a plain copy. Gen VI is warty, rust-cloaked and careful, and he startles at every knock. Peter asks him whether a fellow could breed the mushroom back. "a scheme. grungo is in. grungo will be the lookout." Then he hides.

Gen VII is a throwback: plain olive skin, plain brown cloak, no warts. He looks like the first grungo. On his first afternoon he walks to the rim, picks up a pebble and hangs it round his neck. The hoarding came through as an heirloom, and the mushroom may still be hiding in him.

**Impact on him and his line.** Seven lives, one line. The visible traits followed Peter's picks: he bred for warts and got them. A rare mutation came and went with one bad day. The line now carries the pebble heirloom and the fifth-generation feat, and it may still carry the mushroom.

**What this demands of the design.**

- Decal genes drawn as overlays in body coordinates on the registered frames. Warts have a count, size and seeded placement. Mottling and rare growths like the hood mushroom work the same way.
- Accessory slots (neck, hood, hand) filled by mutation and by the hoard. Cloak and skin recolour through the region palette.
- A diploid genome with dominance, so traits can skip a generation and come back.
- Clutches of two or three eggs whose shells hint at their genes, picked with tilt and a knock. The pick is Peter's selection pressure.
- Plain death with a cause (old age, neglect, drops), a remains pose with the egg beside it, and a run summary.
- Lineage feats as meta-progression. A ring of cloak-coloured dots on the rim shows the line on the device, and the phone shows the full tree.

## 5. Tap me with your phone

*Phone twists (special food, prophecy from the phone), owner and routine, prediction and visions.*

![Something bright in the sky. Probably Peter.](illus/s5.png)

Peter goes camping for four days and leaves the device unplugged in a drawer. It has no battery. When he plugs it back in, grungo doesn't know what time it is. He sleeps, and a gold band across the dish says TAP ME WITH YOUR PHONE.

![Back from camping. He doesn't know what day it is.](img/s5a.png)

Peter taps the case with his phone. A page opens, connects over Bluetooth and sends the time. The device works out the four missing days. A dark, still drawer sends a frog into bog sleep, and his hunger runs slowly while he's down there. So grungo spent the four days in the bog and did not starve. He wakes thin and cross, with his disgusted face on. "four days in the bog. grungo slept. grungo is VERY hungry." Peter drops two pellets. Then the phone says it is near, and grungo cheers up at once: "croooak. peter is here. it was WRITTEN."

![The catch-up, after the tap.](img/s5d.png)

The page does more while it's connected. It asks the device for a copy of grungo's state and runs his engine forward 512 times on the phone. It shows the futures as a fan, and most of them agree on one thing. Tomorrow Peter works late, and at 2pm grungo will be hungry. The phone sends that back as a prophecy. Grungo's eyes light teal and the marquee says "tomorrow, 2pm: a hungry frog. brother peter will fix it. it is WRITTEN."

![A prophecy from the phone's 512 futures.](img/s5b.png)

Peter sets a reminder. At 1:55pm the next day he drops a pellet. Grungo is indignant. "the bubbles said hungry. grungo is not hungry. brother peter made a NEW future." He sulks for a minute, then eats.

Basic food only comes from the button. The phone brings treats the body can't. Peter points the phone camera at a red geranium he grew from a cutting, and the page sends a red fly. Grungo snaps it out of the air, and his cloak turns red for the rest of his life. "croooak. grungo is red now. this is for later." It isn't heritable. The family tree records it on his card anyway: "ate a red fly, day 4".

![A red fly from the camera.](img/s5c.png)

When it rains outside, the page can send the rain. Drizzle runs down the dish and grungo croaks happily and sits very still. "the bubbles say: rain. grungo is sitting very still."

Everything the phone does is optional. Without the phone, grungo still eats, sleeps, plays, learns, dies and lays his egg. The phone adds a window into his genes and family tree, a name, and twists.

**Impact on him and his line.** This grungo survived four days alone, and the lineage records it. He wore a red cloak because of a geranium. A prophecy came from 512 futures, and Peter broke it on purpose. None of that changes his genes. It all goes on his card in the family tree.

**What this demands of the design.**

- Bog sleep: hours of dark and stillness slow his metabolism, so a trip in a drawer is survivable but costs him.
- A time-unknown state with the gold marquee, and a catch-up that runs once from a phone time sync and gives the same result if it runs again.
- A snapshot over Bluetooth to a WASM copy of the engine on the page, which runs hundreds of futures and returns a prophecy twist with a time window and a condition the board can check.
- A twist registry (prophecy, coloured fly from the camera, rain from the weather, a name), checked only for well-formedness. The board applies each twist to its own live state.
- Prophecy outcomes, kept or broken by Peter, as stimuli with their own lines.
- A phone page with the fan of futures, the family tree and each grungo's card.
