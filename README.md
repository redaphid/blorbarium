# blorbarium

Grungo, a small artificial-life frog who lives in a round dish on an ESP32-S3 screen. You care for him with gestures. Each life is a run, and his eggs carry his genes into the next one.

- [What grungo can do](docs/creature.md). His dish, every gesture and button, his senses, actions, drives, faces, learning and life cycle, what the phone adds, and the known limits today.
- [Engine design](docs/design/DESIGN.md) and [deviations from it](docs/design/DEVIATIONS.md), for maintainers.
- [HANDOFF](HANDOFF.md). Where the project started.
